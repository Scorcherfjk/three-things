## Notes

- Timing parsing, ranges, defaults, and the exact rejection wording are unit-tested in `tests/unit/timing.test.ts`, including the invariant that the displayed hints state the very limits the parser enforces.
- The per-round duration mechanism is unit-tested in `tests/unit/roundState.test.ts`: `startRound` derives the deadline and remaining time from the round's own `answerMs`, and the 10 s and 5 min (300 s) extremes are both covered.
- Schema v2 validation and the v1 → v2 upgrade are unit-tested in `tests/unit/sessionStore.test.ts`, including rejection of a v2 document whose stored round has no valid `answerMs` and a non-throwing path when storage is unavailable.
- Countdown accuracy for the configured answer time is asserted for 5, 10, and 300 seconds (_counts down exactly the configured answer time for 5, 10 and 300 seconds (SC-003)_).
- Follow-up for a human reviewer: V12 step 3 (SC-012, no-instructions UX judgement), the V13 Network-tab observation, V7a's full browser restart, and a visual pass at 1280×720 for the new Timing panel.

### Deliberate deviation

Per plan.md §Post-design re-check (research R5, Constitution Principle I): `Round` does **not** persist `animationMs`. The animation phase is fully consumed at the reveal and nothing reads the value afterwards, so persisting it would be dead state; `answerMs` is frozen on the round and is the mechanism that satisfies FR-010. This deviation is disclosed in the pull-request description.
